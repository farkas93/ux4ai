AIPM Toolkit is exposed through a LAN NodePort and requires sign-in in both
LAN and public Gradio-tunnel modes.

Before first use:
  1. Ensure the aipm-secrets Secret exists with the keys configured in values.
  2. Restrict node TCP {{ .Values.service.nodePort }} at the host/upstream firewall to your LAN CIDR.
  3. Wait for the app Pod to become Ready; it runs migrations in its init container.
  4. Sign in at http://<node-ip>:{{ .Values.service.nodePort }}/auth/login.

The optional temporary public Gradio link is controlled from the instructor panel.
Open a new link after an upgrade or rollback replaces the app Pod.
The app permits {{ .Values.app.uiConcurrencyLimit }} concurrent browser callback pipelines,
with each browser's saves and project switches kept sequential.
See docs/CLASSROOM_READINESS.md for a 20-session rehearsal and rollback commands.
